# Key Business Findings

## Revenue & Sales Performance

- **Electronics** remained the largest revenue-contributing category throughout the analysis period (**2018–2023**).
- The category recorded its strongest performance in **2021**, generating approximately **₹485 billion** in revenue.
- Electronics also consistently led in sales volume, contributing approximately **54% of total units sold** every year, making it the highest-selling category across the entire period.

## Geographic Insights

- **Pune** emerged as the most consistent revenue-generating city, ranking among the **Top 4 revenue-contributing cities** every year.
- **Delhi** followed closely as the **second most consistent** city, maintaining a strong contribution to overall revenue throughout the analysis period.

## Pricing Analysis

- The **Average Selling Price (ASP)** remained remarkably consistent across all product categories, ranging between **₹23,000 and ₹24,000**, indicating a balanced pricing strategy across the portfolio.

## Brand Performance

- **Nike** emerged as the highest overall revenue-generating brand during **2018–2023**.
- **Puma** demonstrated strong consistency by:
  - Ranking among the **Top 3 brands overall**, and
  - Securing a **Top 3 annual revenue position** in **2 out of 6 years**.

## Seller Performance

- **UrbanRetails** was the highest overall revenue-contributing seller during **2018–2023**.
- It also ranked among the **Top 3 sellers** in **5 out of 6 years**, making it the most consistent high-performing seller.
- **QuickShop** was the second most consistent seller, appearing in the **Top 3 sellers** **3 times** while also ranking among the **overall Top 3 revenue-contributing sellers**.

## Customer Ratings

- The **Mobiles** category consistently achieved the highest average product rating (approximately **3.0**) across all years.
- **Sports** ranked second, maintaining an average rating close to **3.0** throughout the analysis period.

## Discount Analysis

- The average discount across all product categories remained stable at approximately **20–21%**, indicating a consistent promotional strategy.
- Despite similar discount levels across categories, **Electronics** ranked among the **Top 2 highest-discount categories** in **4 out of 6 years**, while simultaneously remaining the **highest revenue-generating category**.
